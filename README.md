# Hey, I'm Ankit 👋

Backend engineer in Noida. I've gone from freelancing in college to a startup that didn't make it, then COBOL for a bank, and now I build systems that handle a billion clicks a day. Here's how that happened.

---

## 🛤️ How I got here

**2017 · Patna, college and freelancing**
Started a BCA at ISM Patna and spent most of college building things for real clients: websites for around **15 freelance clients**, plus **Upchaaar**, a site for ordering medicines and booking nearby doctors and ambulances. Plain PHP and MySQL, but it was the first time something I built felt useful.

**College · Trying the startup route**
Joined **Agiro** as its first engineer. It ran **Agiro Cabs**, a self-drive car rental service, and **Agiro Tech**, which taught programming and digital marketing. It didn't take off, but it taught me more about shipping, users, and running out of runway than any classroom did.

**Feb 2021 · First job, in a newsroom**
My corporate journey started as a web developer at **The Probe**, a digital news organisation, working on their website for a few months.

**Jul 2021 · Then, a mainframe**
Joined **Cognizant** and spent the next year writing **COBOL and JCL** for a big US bank. Yes, really. It taught me early that a slow batch job costs real money. I also started an online MCA alongside work and finished it in 2023.

**Nov 2022 · GeeksforGeeks**
Switched to modern backend development at the company whose articles got a lot of us through college. I built the systems behind their email and WhatsApp campaigns. My favourite win was sending a million emails in 45 minutes instead of 3 hours.

**Sep 2024 · Trackier**
Went from sending a million emails to handling a billion clicks a day. I work on the fraud detection and deduplication that filters out fake and duplicate clicks, in a Go service on Kafka, MongoDB and ClickHouse. I also built automated daily report delivery for clients, and brought AI-assisted development into our team's workflow.
